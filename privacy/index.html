Privacy Policy

Plain-language privacy information for this website.

Site: morethanjustvibes.com Operator (Controller): Nicole Hessner Contact: nikki@nikkihessner.com Last updated: 2026-01-26

Overview

This is a personal blog documenting how I use AI to improve my life and build coding projects. The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages.

Quick summary

  • No analytics are enabled at this time.
  • No cookies are used by this site at this time.
  • No accounts, no comments, and no contact forms are provided on-site at this time.
  • Like most websites, the hosting provider may process basic server logs (for example, IP address and request metadata) for security and operations.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly

At this time, this site does not provide forms, accounts, or comment features, so I do not collect personal information directly through the website.

Information collected automatically (server logs)

When you visit the site, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages and its infrastructure) may process limited technical information in server logs for security and operational purposes. This can include:

  • IP address
  • Device and browser type (user agent)
  • Pages requested and the date/time of access
  • Referring page (if provided by your browser)

I do not attempt to identify you from this information, and I do not merge it with other data sources.

How we use information

Any technical information processed (primarily via hosting logs) is used for:

  • Serving site content
  • Maintaining site reliability and performance
  • Detecting and preventing abuse, fraud, and security incidents
  • Complying with legal obligations if required

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, pixels, localStorage tracking, fingerprinting, or behavioral advertising technology at this time.

If I add analytics, embedded media, a comment system, a newsletter, or a contact form in the future, I will update this policy before (or when) changes go live and provide any required choices/controls.

Sharing of information

I do not sell your personal information.

I do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising (sometimes described as “sharing” under certain laws).

Hosting providers and related infrastructure vendors may process limited technical data as part of providing the website (for example, delivering content and preventing abuse).

Third parties and recipients

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process limited technical data (such as IP address and request metadata) to host and deliver the website and to maintain security and operational reliability.

Learn more: GitHub Privacy Statement.

International data transfers

Depending on where you live, your information may be processed in countries outside your jurisdiction as part of website hosting and delivery. Hosting providers may operate globally and use infrastructure in multiple regions.

For details about GitHub’s processing and transfers, please refer to: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data retention

I do not store personal information collected directly through this site at this time.

Any server-log data handled by the hosting provider is retained according to the hosting provider’s practices and operational requirements. I do not control the hosting provider’s log retention settings.

Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK visitors)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, GDPR/UK GDPR may apply. To the extent the hosting of this site involves processing personal data (such as IP addresses in server logs), the processing is based on legitimate interests in operating and securing the website and providing content.

Because this site does not collect personal information directly, requests will generally result in confirmation that I do not hold personal data about you, except possibly in hosting provider logs that I do not control.

California notice: This site does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
